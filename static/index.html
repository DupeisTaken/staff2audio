FROM PAGE TO PRACTICE

A small guide to Prima

  1. Bring a clear score.

    Upload a PDF or image, drag it in, or paste a screenshot. Keep all five staff lines and the clef visible. Crop out other windows; keep the staff level.

  2. Pick your melody.

    Each detected staff is a separate practice phrase. For PDFs, choose a page. Filled markers show detected notes; amber means the reading needs a closer look.

  3. Listen, review, repeat.

    Play the sampled grand piano, slow the tempo, transpose, or loop. Click notes to audition; use Review notes to correct pitches and rhythm. MIDI and WAV downloads use your corrections.

Best with clean, printed, monophonic treble or bass staves. Chords, multiple voices, ties, tuplets, ornaments, lyrics, handwriting, and perspective photos are not reliably interpreted. Key names show major / relative minor because the signature alone cannot determine the mode. Recognition estimates are heuristics, not calibrated accuracy percentages.

Entirely local. No account. No score uploads.
